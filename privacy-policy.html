Privacy Policy for Mafia

Android app “Mafia” (package com.niyutshah.mafia) by Niyut Shah
Effective date: 8 November 2026

In short: Mafia does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your phone, and the app has no internet access.

Information you enter

To play, you type the players’ names. These names are kept only in the phone’s memory while a game is running. They are never saved to storage, never sent anywhere, and they disappear when you close the app.

No internet, no accounts, no tracking

  • The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any information off your phone.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, advertisements, analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases.
  • The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

Narration (text-to-speech)

When the app’s narrator reads the game aloud, the narration lines (which can include player names) are passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, such as Google’s Speech Services. That engine is part of your phone’s system and is covered by its provider’s own privacy policy. You can turn narration off at any time with the sound button.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission only: Vibration, used for short haptic feedback during the game. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Backups

The app keeps no data that needs backing up, and it opts out of Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

Children

Mafia is a party game intended for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
